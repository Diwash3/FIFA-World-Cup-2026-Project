FIFA World Cup 2026 Project - Raw Data Package

FIFA_Official/
- Contains the FIFA 2026 CSV files uploaded for team-level and player-level statistics.
- Keep these files unchanged in data/raw/ for the assessment.

FBref/
- Contains the uploaded FBref 2026 World Cup Standard Stats PDF used as source evidence.
- The squad standard stats shown on pages 2-3 include fields such as Squad, MP, CrdY and CrdR.

Important:
- Do not manually clean the raw files in Excel.
- Perform data wrangling, sampling, statistics and visualisation in Python as required by the assessment.
